# world-happiness-data-analysis
Tableau data analysis of the World Happiness Report, exploring GDP, social support, life expectancy, freedom, corruption, and global happiness trends.
# Project Overview
This project analyses global happiness data to explore the key factors associated with happiness across different countries.
## Objectives
- Identify the happiest and least happy countries
- Analyse factors associated with happiness
- Compare GDP and happiness levels across countries
- Explore the relationship between social support and happiness
- Examine life expectancy and freedom
- Analyse corruption-related patterns
- Compare happiness indicators before and after the pandemic

##  Tools Used
- Tableau
- Data Visualisation
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Descriptive and Bivariate Analysis
- Microsoft PowerPoint

## Analysis

The project includes visualisations covering:

- Top 10 happiest countries
- Least happy countries
- Life expectancy
- Social support
- Freedom
- GDP per capita
- Corruption
- Impact of the pandemic
- Before and after pandemic comparisons

##  Key Insights

The analysis indicates that happiness is associated with a combination of economic stability, social support, trust and healthcare access.

Countries such as Finland and Denmark demonstrate a combination of strong economies, social systems and lower perceived corruption.

The pandemic analysis also highlights the importance of social resilience and community support in maintaining well-being during periods of crisis.

##  Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project highlights several areas for improving well-being:

- Improve access to healthcare and preventive care
- Strengthen social and community support
- Promote economic stability and employment opportunities
- Improve transparency and institutional trust
- Encourage community initiatives and volunteering
